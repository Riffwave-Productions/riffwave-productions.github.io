Last updated: August 29, 2026

Riffwave Productions ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our TikTok application and related services (the "Service").

When you authorize our application via TikTok Login Kit, we may receive basic profile information (such as display name, avatar, and account identifiers) and permissions to publish or manage content on your behalf, limited to the scopes you explicitly approve during authorization.

We use the information solely to operate the Service: publishing content, managing posts, and reporting basic analytics for the connected TikTok account(s). We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Access tokens and account information are stored securely and used only to perform actions you have explicitly authorized. We take reasonable technical measures to protect this data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention

We retain account connection data only for as long as necessary to provide the Service. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings, which will disconnect our application's access to your account.

Our Service uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit / Content Posting API) to operate. Your use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request data deletion, contact us via our official channels listed on our TikTok profile.
